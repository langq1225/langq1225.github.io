I am a second-year M.S. student in the [School of Electrical Engineering](https://ee.kaist.ac.kr/en/) at [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/) (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology), advised by Prof. [Insu Han](https://insuhan.github.io/). Previously, I obtained my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology at [Beijing University of Technology](https://english.bjut.edu.cn/).

I have a broad research interest in **Efficient AI** and **AI Systems**. Currently, I focus on **Looped Transformers**.
